&#x20;                        **Prevention Checklist** 



* Never share private photos or sensitive information with anyone not even if you trust them.



* Turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on all the apps you use.



* Keep your social media accounts "Private" and accept the follow/friend requests of those only whom you know in real life.



* Never click on random links in your messages.



* Remember never give or enter your PIN to receive money.



* If things get scary or out of control, report it to the National Cyber Crime Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or call the Helpline 1930 .
